02 / Work

Work

Yauvana Estate

Premium plotted land · Thirthahalli, Malnad

Self-initiated project

The problem

Land buyers in Malnad cannot see what they are buying.

Plots are sold from a paper layout and a site visit that eats a whole day. Most property sites in the region are a photo gallery and a phone number. They show the land as it is today — trees, mud, a boundary stone. They do not show what it becomes.

So the buyer is asked to imagine, and then to trust. Most of them do neither.

The decision

Photographs were the wrong proof.

You cannot photograph a house that has not been built. For plotted land, the product is the future, not the present — which means renders and a 3D model are the honest way to show it, not decoration on top of photos.

That one decision shaped the whole build.

What was built

1
Land that reveals itself

A scroll-driven image sequence that moves through the estate as you read, instead of sitting in a gallery.

2
A layout you can turn

An interactive 3D layout model — buyers look at it from any angle, instead of squinting at a flat PDF.

3
Enquiries, straight to the seller

An enquiry form wired to the seller. No dashboard, no middleman.

4
Motion that feels considered

Built with GSAP and Lenis, so scrolling never feels mechanical.

04 / How it works

How it works

No surprises. You know the price, the plan and the date before any money changes hands.

01

1. We talk — free

A call or a WhatsApp conversation. Twenty minutes, usually less.

We ask what your business does, who your customers are, and what you want this site to actually achieve. No pitch, no pressure.

Costs you nothing.

02

2. You get a written quote — free

Within two days you get one message with everything in it: what we are building, what is included, anything extra you chose, one final number, and the date it will be finished.

That number does not change later.

Costs you nothing.

03

3. You pay 25% — we start

Once you are happy with the quote, 25% up front and the work begins. UPI, bank transfer, or PayPal if you are outside India.

A quarter to start, so you are not handing over much before you have seen anything. The rest is spread across the build.

This is the point where planning, design and writing start properly.

04

4. You send your content — we plan around it

Photos, logo, menu, prices, anything you already have. Send it while we plan, not after, because what you have decides how the site is built.

Do not worry about quality. We will tell you what is usable, what needs reshooting, and what we can fix ourselves.

Your date starts when your content arrives. This is the one thing that delays projects, and it is almost never the developer.

05

5. You see it before anyone else

You get a private link to the real site, working, on your phone and your laptop. Not a picture of a website. The actual thing.

Tell us what to change. Your plan includes rounds of changes, and we use them properly — one clear list at a time, not fifty messages.

06

6. Your own name

Not a free subdomain. Not someone else’s address with your name tacked on. yourbusiness.com, and it belongs to you.

We handle the whole setup. You pay for the domain directly, in your own name, and we get your first year at a discount.

07

7. Final payment, then it goes live

The remaining balance, and your site is live on your domain the same day.

You get everything: the files, the accounts, the logins. All in your name. If you ever want to work with someone else, nothing is locked and nothing is held.

08

8. 45 days of free changes

Launch day is not the finish line. For 45 days we change whatever you want — text, photos, prices, hours. No invoice.

Anything broken stays free forever.

Most sites take two to five weeks from advance to launch, depending on size and how fast your content arrives. Your exact date is in your quote.
