# Content plan — THE WEBESSY STUDIOS

Fill this in before any design work. Answer in plain sentences.
Bad writing you actually wrote beats good writing you are still planning.

Version 1 is **one page** with sections, not five separate pages.
Less to write, less to maintain, easier to send as a single link.

---

## 1. The one line

The first thing anyone reads. What you do, for whom.

> I build ______________ for ______________ in ______________.

Bad: "Crafting digital experiences that elevate brands."
Good: "I build fast, modern websites for small businesses in Bengaluru."

Say it the way you would say it out loud to someone at a wedding.

**Your answer:**

**Supporting line** (availability, proof, or a reason to trust you):

---

## 2. Work — the most important section

This is the section that gets you hired. Everything else is supporting.

For **each** project, answer all five:

1. **Name of the project or client**
2. **What was the problem?** What did they have before? What was not working?
3. **What did you build?** Pages, features, anything unusual
4. **What changed?** Enquiries, bookings, time saved, anything measurable. If you have no numbers, say what the client said.
5. **Images** — 3 to 5 screenshots. Desktop and phone.

Write it as a short story, not a feature list. Problem, what you did, what happened.

### Project 1
### Project 2
### Project 3

**Minimum three.** Two feels thin. One looks like an accident.

---

## 3. Services — what you actually sell

List 3 or 4. For each:

- **Name of the service**
- **Who it is for**
- **What they get** (pages, revisions, handover, training)
- **How long it takes**
- **Price, or a starting price**

Starter ideas, change these:
- One-page site for a small business
- Multi-page business site
- Redesign of an existing site
- Maintenance and updates, monthly

### On pricing

Publishing a starting price filters out people who were never going to pay, before they take three hours of your time. That is the whole reason to do it.

"From ₹X" is enough. You do not have to publish a full price list.

**Research this before you decide:** search what other freelancers and small studios in Bengaluru charge. Look at 10. Note the range. Price near the middle at first, not at the bottom. Cheap attracts the worst clients.

---

## 4. Process — how working with you goes

Clients are scared of three things: it will take forever, it will cost more than quoted, and they will not be able to change anything afterwards.

Answer all three here, in 4 or 5 steps.

> 1. We talk — call or WhatsApp, 30 minutes, free
> 2. I send a quote and a timeline
> 3. ...
> 4. ...
> 5. Handover — you get the files and I show you how to update it

This section costs you an hour and closes more clients than your design will.

---

## 5. About — who you are

Not a CV. Three short paragraphs at most.

- Who you are and where you are based
- Why you build websites
- One human detail

**Add a real photo of your face.** For a solo freelancer in India, a face is a trust signal. A logo is not a person, and small business owners hire people.

What `0400` means goes here. If there is a story about the hour, this is where it lives.

---

## 6. Contact — what happens next

- WhatsApp number, as a click-to-chat link
- Email
- A short form: name, what they need, budget range
- **Say how fast you reply.** "I reply within one working day." Small promise, big effect.

Do not put a phone number you will not answer.

---

## 7. Trust elements — scatter these through the page

- **Testimonials.** Ask everyone you have built for. Two sentences each is plenty. Name and business name. This is the single highest-value thing you can collect this week.
- Number of projects delivered
- Client logos, if you have permission
- Where you are based — local matters for local clients

## 8. Footer

- Copyright and year
- Links back to the sections
- Instagram and LinkedIn if you use them
- Email

---

## 9. SEO fields — one set per page

- **Page title** — under 60 characters, must contain what you do and where
- **Meta description** — under 155 characters, written for a human, not a search engine
- **Alt text** for every image, describing what is in it

---

## What to research before you write

1. **Ten competitor sites.** Bengaluru web designers and small studios. Note: what they offer, how they price, what they show first, what annoys you about them.
2. **Pricing.** What does a small business site cost here? Find the real range.
3. **Your likely clients' current websites.** What is actually wrong with them? Slow, no phone number, broken on mobile? That list becomes your services section.
4. **What your clients search for.** Not "web developer". More likely "website for my shop", "website design price Bangalore".
5. **Testimonials.** Message everyone you have built anything for. Do this today, because replies take days to arrive.

---

## Order to write in

1. The one line
2. Work, all three projects
3. Contact
4. Services and pricing
5. Process
6. About
7. SEO fields

Work first, because it is the hardest and most valuable. If you run out of energy after that, you still have a site worth showing.
